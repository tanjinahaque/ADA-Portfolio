About Me

I'm an accessibility professional with 8+ years of experience helping teams create more inclusive digital experiences. My work brings together accessibility research, interaction design, technical evaluation, and cross-functional collaboration to help organizations make better design decisions.

I specialize in researching complex interaction patterns, translating accessibility requirements into practical design recommendations, and developing reusable component guidance for designers and developers. I've worked across banking, technology, and healthcare, contributing to accessible experiences on web and native mobile platforms.

Today, I'm expanding my work in UX/UI design, design systems, and human-centered product strategy. Through independent design projects, I'm applying my accessibility expertise to interface design, Figma components, and end-to-end user experiences—with the goal of making accessibility an integral part of how products are designed, not something addressed only after development.

Skills & Expertise

  • Accessibility Testing & Auditing

    Manual and automated accessibility evaluations across web and native mobile platforms. Expertise includes keyboard navigation, JAWS, NVDA, VoiceOver, TalkBack, WCAG conformance evaluation, defect reporting, and remediation validation.

  • Inclusive Design & Accessibility

    Reviewing designs, evaluating accessible interaction patterns, identifying barriers early, and recommending practical solutions that improve inclusive user experiences.

  • Design Systems Accessibility

    Creating reusable component accessibility guidance, including keyboard interactions, screen reader behavior, component states, accessibility specifications, acceptance criteria, and implementation examples.

  • Interaction Research

    Researching complex interaction patterns, synthesizing existing usability findings and accessibility evidence, and translating research into actionable recommendations for designers and product teams.

  • UX/UI Design

    Applying user-centered design methods through independent projects, including wireframing, interface design, visual hierarchy, prototyping, and reusable Figma components.

  • Product Collaboration & Influence

    Partnering with designers, developers, and product owners to evaluate competing approaches, communicate technical requirements, facilitate decisions, and provide accessibility education.
